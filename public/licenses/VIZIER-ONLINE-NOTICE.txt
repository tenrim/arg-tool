CDS/VizieR online catalogue data
Source: https://vizier.cds.unistra.fr/
Service terms: https://cds.unistra.fr/legals/

The original site adapter makes explicit read-only ASU requests for catalogue
metadata, table schema or bounded selected rows. It does not bundle a catalogue
mirror or VizieR server software. A result window is not the complete database.

Credit: CDS/VizieR and each catalogue's original authors, publication and DOI.
All returned metadata, units, field definitions, reference and rights_uri values
are retained. Licence conditions are dataset-specific; the CDS website terms do
not replace the individual catalogue's licence. Records must not be labelled as
uniformly CC0 or as a single site's original scientific measurements.
Bundled original service terms: CDS-ONLINE-TERMS.txt.
