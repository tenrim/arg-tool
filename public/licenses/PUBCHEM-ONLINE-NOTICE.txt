PubChem online data
Source: https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/
Providers: National Center for Biotechnology Information, National Library of
Medicine, National Institutes of Health, and the contributors identified in the
returned PubChem records and PUG View references.
Policies: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/

The original site adapter uses explicit PUG REST and PUG View requests for
compound identifiers, complete records, annotations, synonyms and 2D/3D structure
records. No PubChem server software or local curated substitute is bundled.

NCBI aggregates material from several data providers. Do not infer that every
annotation, chemical datum or referenced image is CC0 or free of third-party
rights. Keep CID, the original references, contributor information, conditions,
source links and retrieval time. Follow the licence of each material's provider.
Bundled original NCBI policies: PUBCHEM-POLICIES.txt.
