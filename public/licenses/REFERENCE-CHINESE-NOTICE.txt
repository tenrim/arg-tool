Airports: Chinese labels joined by ICAO (P239) / IATA (P238); multiple observed Chinese identities or conflicting ICAO/IATA identities are rejected. At least one source coordinate must be within 15 km of the original airport.
Capitals: country ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 (P297) plus capital (P36), with an exact normalized English source capital name. This supplements names; it does not reclassify countries or assert geopolitical status.
UN/LOCODE: all A–Z prefixes queried automatically, zh labels only. Exact P1937 codes match existing locations. Multiple observed Chinese item identities for a code are rejected. Codes refer to transportation locations, not only seaports.
Deep sky: catalog-code statements P528 qualified by NGC (Q14534), IC (Q741672) or Messier (Q14530); Chinese labels and aliases. Exact normalized identifiers only; multiple observed Chinese identities are rejected. No translation or new astronomical object is invented.

Coverage is partial: missing labels, ambiguous joins or failed coordinate/name validation retain the original names. Wikidata is a community-maintained structured-data source, not an official naming authority; labels can contain upstream errors. All original names, identifiers and raw factual fields remain available.

The 21 geological names with absent Chinese labels are generated using lower/middle/upper and the pinned ICS chart's existing Chinese parent name. This is an original deterministic naming transformation, not Wikidata content. Original ages, ranks and parent IDs remain unchanged.
